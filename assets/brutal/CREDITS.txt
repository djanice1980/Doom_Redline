REDLINE community gore pack
===========================

Everything in this folder was taken from two community projects and is used
by REDLINE's Brutal mode (OPTIONS > BRUTAL). None of it is covered by
REDLINE's MIT licence. It is bundled with credit, under each project's own
terms, so the game works out of the box; the folder is self-contained and can
be deleted or left out of a release at any time. Without it the game falls
back to the blood, pool and puff sprites of the player's own Doom WAD. Should
any author object, the folder will be removed.

Loading: REDLINE looks for this folder next to the executable ("brutal"), in
share/redline/brutal, or in assets/brutal of a source checkout; the
REDLINE_BRUTAL_PACK environment variable points it elsewhere.
