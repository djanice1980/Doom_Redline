Material maps for the three Doom textures REDLINE uses (STARTAN3 wall,
FLOOR4_8 floor, CEIL3_5 ceiling): a normal map and a roughness map each,
KTX2 (BC7 / RGBA8, with mips).

Taken from the GZDoom: Ray Traced project by Vitaly Shirokii,
https://github.com/vs-shirokii/gzdoom-rt (release 1.0.2, rt/mat/), which
generated them from id Software's original textures. They are derived from
id Software art and are not covered by REDLINE's MIT licence. The game only
uses them when the player's own Doom WAD is loaded; they add nothing without
the textures they were made from. If the rights holders object, these six
files will be removed.
